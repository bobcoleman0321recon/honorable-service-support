Honorable Service Guide

A training and remedial program for New Jersey public pension staff and investigators who prepare honorable service reviews under N.J.S.A. 43:1-3. It teaches the eleven-factor analysis on fictional training files, with a refresher checklist, knowledge checks and supervisor quality control. It runs on iPhone and iPad and works offline.

Not endorsed, not legal advice. This is an independent training aid. It is not published by or on behalf of the State of New Jersey, the Department of the Treasury, the Division of Pensions and Benefits, PFRSNJ or any board. Nothing in it is legal advice.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection? No. Everything is built into the app and works offline.

Where are my practice answers and supervisor records kept? Only on your device. The app has no server and sends nothing anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I move supervisor records to another device? On the Supervisor page, export a records file and import it on the other device. You choose where the file goes.

How do I clear everything? Delete the app. That removes all of its data from the device.

Are the cases real? No. Every training file is fictional. Never enter a real member's name, Social Security number or case details anywhere in the app.
