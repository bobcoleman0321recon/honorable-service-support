Privacy policy

Honorable Service Guide

Effective November 10, 2026

The app collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app saves what you enter so you can pick up where you left off:

  • practice answers, quiz answers and checklist marks;
  • on the Supervisor page, review findings, assignments and sign-offs.

These are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The developer cannot see them, and they are not sent anywhere.

Files you export

The Supervisor page can export records to a file so you can move them to another device. The file goes only where you send it, and what happens to it after that is up to you and your organization's rules.

What not to enter

All training files are fictional. Don't enter a real member's name, Social Security number, case details or health information anywhere in the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is a professional training aid and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's GitHub page. Issues are public, so don't include personal information.
